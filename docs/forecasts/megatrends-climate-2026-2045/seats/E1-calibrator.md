## Reasoning

Its 2018-23 bars, with 2024 at 2.9/2.8 (the premortem's search summary, not re-verified), compound to 0.71 for 2018-24: the premortem's arithmetic reproduces. Adding 2025 (4.6/2.9) and the WTO's 2026-27 forecasts ([8 Oct 2026](https://www.wto.org/english/news_e/news26_e/stat_08oct26_510_e.htm)), the same series gives 0.83 for 2018-25 and 0.96 for 2018-27 (my arithmetic; short windows with a pandemic inside; 2027 "depends on a timely resolution of the Middle East conflict"). So the hit is real: 0.67 for eighteen years is close to what a seven-year tariff-era window delivered, not a far tail. But the premortem's 0.84 removes the AI years, and the lock's window opens with two of them. Eighteen-year trend 0.82, plus 0.07 from the head start: 0.89. P(E < 0.75) = 26%.
