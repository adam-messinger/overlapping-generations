# Growth backcast 1990-2025

Generated by `scripts/growth-backcast.ts`. The forward production
structure (exergy-weighted energy, endogenous Wright’s-law + education
efficiency, no exogenous TFP) is anchored in 1990 and driven with
observed world capital, labor, electricity, final energy, and education
through 2025. If the structure is right, it should reproduce observed
growth; the shortfall measures the growth engine the model is missing.

Higher γ fits history better (useful energy grew faster than the
α/β-weighted K,L bundle), but even γ=0.70 leaves a large gap — and
closing the gap entirely on γ would need γ ≈ 1, far outside any
defended range. The missing engine is not the energy elasticity.

## Caveats

- Observed series are approximate (±5-10%); the measured shortfall is
  several times larger than that uncertainty.
- Labor uses working-age headcount; education enters via collegeShare →
  organizational efficiency, mirroring the model’s own separation.
  If effective (education-weighted) labor grew ~0.3 pp/yr faster, the
  β=0.15 weight makes that worth only ~0.05 pp/yr of the gap.
- Damages, energy-burden, food stress, and system overheads are zeroed
  on both sides of the comparison (small, roughly constant shares).
- 2020 covid dip is in the observed GDP series; per-decade rows bracket it.
